Imagine a small tea stall beside a campus gate. The regulars rarely pay in cash. Instead, the owner
writes every order in a worn notebook called the khata, and people settle up later.
Jamal Mama runs the stall. One evening Nabila orders tea and singara and says, “Put it on the khata.”
Later Tuhin offers to cover Nabila's bill. Tuhin then asks Ishrat to take it over, and Ishrat passes it on to
Rafi.
At the end of the month, Jamal Mama asks Nabila to clear her bill.
Nabila: “Tuhin said he would pay it.”
Tuhin: “Ishrat took it over from me.”
Ishrat: “Rafi agreed to settle it.”
Rafi: “I already sent the money to Mama.”
Jamal Mama: “I never received it.”
Now think: Who actually owes Jamal Mama the money? Who is responsible for paying it? How can a
payment be confirmed? What if two people claim they paid the same bill?
Your task: Build a JavaScript system that tracks people, their actions, their responsibilities, and the
history of every khata entry, without losing important information.
